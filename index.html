Po-Han Chen

Hi, I’m Po-Han. I’m currently looking for roles in digital IC design, including architecture, verification, and physical design.

I recently completed my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at Stanford University, advised by Professor Priyanka Raina. My research focused on coarse-grained reconfigurable array (CGRA) architectures and high-performance, energy-efficient computing systems.

Before Stanford, I completed my M.S. at National Tsing Hua University under Professor Chao-Tsung Huang, where I designed ASICs for digital refocusing. I then worked as a Design Engineer at MediaTek, developing hardware for image signal processing (ISP) pipelines.
